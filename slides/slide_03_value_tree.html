The Value Tree

Before discussing AI agents, we anchor on the 4 P&L and operational levers that drive TM&D’s commercial performance across General Trade, Rural, Modern Trade, and Quick Commerce.

North Star Outcome

Profitable Revenue Growth & Capital Efficiency across TM&D

Lever 01 · Revenue

Top-Line Growth & Assortment Mix

Higher shelf availability, sharper outlet-level cross-sell & premiumization, and faster new product (NPD) placement across GT, Rural, MT, and Q-Com.

↑ Secondary Sales & LPC
Lever 02 · Gross Margin

Trade Spend & Margin Effectiveness

Moving from blanket schemes to hyper-targeted promotions—eliminating leakage and boosting incremental ROI per rupee of trade spend.

↑ Promo ROI & Realization
Lever 03 · Cost-to-Serve

Distribution & Logistics Cost Efficiency

Higher truck fill rates, lower freight % of sales, reduced MT/Q-Com fill-rate penalties, and lower expiry & write-offs.

↓ Freight % & OTIF Fines
Lever 04 · Cash & Productivity

Working Capital & Field/Back-Office Productivity

Faster distributor cash-to-cash, automated claim & freight settlement, and more selling time for field reps and managers.

↓ DSI & ↑ Selling Hours

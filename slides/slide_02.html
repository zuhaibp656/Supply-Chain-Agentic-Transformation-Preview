Why TM&D Execution Is Mission-Critical—And Where Friction Creeps In

Across a multi-category FMCG portfolio (Foods, Personal Care, Stationery, Tobacco) moving from Plant → CFA → Wholesale Dealer → 7M+ Kirana, Modern Trade & Q-Commerce outlets, even a 1–2% gap in fill rate or trade spend efficiency directly impacts revenue and margin.

15–20%Kirana Out-of-Stock (OOS) recoverable via predictive replenishment
12–15%Trade scheme & display claim leakage from manual POD/photo audits
4–6 DaysWorking capital locked in imbalanced CFA & Wholesale Dealer stock
Challenge 01 · Beat & Assortment Execution

Kirana OOS & SFA App Fatigue

DSRs carry 60+ SKUs across cluttered SFA screens and default to top-3 fast movers—missing high-margin NPD & focus-SKU placement. ASMs spend 90+ mins daily slicing MIS sheets.

Challenge 02 · CFA Allocation & Channel SLAs

CFA Rationing & Q-Comm Penalties

One branch faces short-dated SLOB write-offs while a neighboring CFA rations stock. Meanwhile, Blinkit, Zepto, and DMart enforce strict >75% shelf-life and dock-slot OTIF penalties.

Challenge 03 · Trade Claims & Freight Audit

Claims, Window Displays & Detention

Paper PODs, damaged biscuit/soap returns, window-display proofs, and transporter detention claims take weeks to reconcile—creating WD friction and silent margin leakage.

TM&D Leadership Example · Pre-Festive North & West Branch Surge
Ahead of Diwali, secondary demand for 5kg Atta & Premium Biscuits spikes +22% in Lucknow while Pune holds 18 days of excess stock. Today, Area Managers, CS&L, and Branch Finance spend days on calls and spreadsheets to re-allocate CFA stock, protect Modern Trade slots, and settle WD claims—whereas an Agentic TM&D overlay detects the shift 7–14 days early, proposes fair-share WD splits, and verifies display/POD claims in hours.

Why “Agentic” Now for TM&D?

TM&D already leads the industry in distribution reach and digital foundations. The next leap is closing the gap between seeing an issue on a dashboard and executing the fix across systems.

01 · Today's Strength

Unmatched Scale, Synergy & Digital Maturity

  • Massive Reach & Digital Spine: Serving ~7M retail outlets with direct servicing, integrated CFA & stockist portals, and deep digital analytics.
  • Multi-Category & Omnichannel Synergy: Moving a diverse portfolio (Foods, Personal Care, Stationery, Tobacco) across General Trade, Modern Trade, and Quick Commerce from one unified backbone.
02 · The Execution Bottleneck

Insight Is Automated; Action Is Still Manual

  • Manual Cross-System Stitching: Dashboards already flag low fill rates, truck detention, and regional stock-outs—but humans still stitch together fixes across ERP, TMS/WMS, transporters, CFAs, and WDs.
  • Channel SLA & Margin Pressure: Balancing strict Modern Trade & Q-Commerce rules (>75% shelf-life, tight dock slots, OTIF penalties) alongside manual trade-claim audits silently erodes margin.
The Paradigm Shift in Operational AI
Era 1 · Past

Traditional Automation

Follows rigid, hard-coded rules. Works only for predictable flows and breaks the moment an exception occurs.

→
Era 2 · Recent

GenAI Copilots

Answer questions and summarize reports on demand, but remain passive—still waiting for humans to decide and act.

→
Era 3 · Now (Agentic AI)

Autonomous Execution with Guardrails

Continuously monitors live signals, reasons across constraints (cost, shelf-life, channel SLAs), takes bounded action across systems, and escalates only true exceptions.
